Privacy Policy

Effective Date: March 25, 2026
This Privacy Policy explains how Roll Cookbook ("the App", "we", "us") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our recipe management application. By using Roll Cookbook, you agree to the practices described in this policy.
1

Information We Collect

👤
Username
Your chosen username identifies your account and associates your recipes with your profile. Usernames are visible to other users of the App.
🍽️
Recipe Data
Recipes you add, import, or create — including titles, ingredients, instructions, images, ratings, and notes.
📊
Usage Data
Information about how you interact with the App, including favourited recipes, collections, meal plans, shopping lists, and your friends list.
🖼️
Images
Photos you upload for recipes are stored in Google Drive associated with the App's Google account.
2

How We Store Your Data

Roll Cookbook stores all user data in Google Sheets and Google Drive, managed through Google Apps Script. Data is stored on Google's servers and is subject to Google's infrastructure security measures.

Your data is stored in a backend service accessible only to the App. We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

3

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide and operate the App's core features (recipe storage, meal planning, shopping lists)
  • Enable social features such as following other users, sharing recipes, and notifications
  • Improve the App's performance and user experience
  • Allow you to share recipes publicly via shareable links at your discretion
  • Power recipe discovery search results
4

Third-Party Services

Roll Cookbook uses the following third-party services to operate:

Google Apps Script & Google Drive
Used for backend data storage and image hosting. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
SerpAPI
Used to power the recipe discovery search feature. Search queries you enter are sent to SerpAPI to retrieve results. Subject to SerpAPI's Privacy Policy.
Google Gemini API
Used to extract recipe data from web pages when importing recipes by URL. Page content may be sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
5

Public Recipe Sharing

If you choose to generate a public shareable link for a recipe, that recipe's content — including title, ingredients, instructions, and image — will be accessible to anyone with the link, without requiring a login.

You control whether to generate such links. We do not publicly index or publish your recipes without your explicit action.

6

Data Retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. If you wish to have your account and associated data deleted, please contact us using the information below. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7

Children's Privacy

Roll Cookbook is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with information, please contact us so we can remove it promptly.

8

Security

Note: Roll Cookbook currently uses a username-only authentication system without passwords. We recommend using a username that does not contain personal information, and only sharing your username with people you trust.

We take reasonable measures to protect your information through Google's infrastructure security. However, no system is completely secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security of your data.

9

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes by updating the effective date at the top of this document. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
